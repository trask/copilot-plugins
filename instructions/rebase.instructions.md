# Rebase / Merge Conflict Resolution

- Resolve conflicts by preserving the intent of both sides, not by blindly picking one side.
- Use `git log`, `git show`, or `git diff` to examine history and understand the intent behind each conflicting change before resolving.
- When intent from both sides can coexist, combine them. When they contradict, ask for clarification.
